# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Early-stage founders preparing for investor conversations. Context: they have an idea, limited time, and need pressure-tested narrative plus investor-readable documents (projections, market sizing, deal structure), not encouragement.

## Product Purpose

WeStartup is an AI advisor that challenges weak assumptions, then produces investor-ready analytics and documents. Success: founder leaves clearer and document-ready in under an hour.

## Brand Personality

Skeptical, precise, calm. Investor-room energy without cruelty. Speaks like diligence, not brainstorming.

## Anti-references

- Indigo/purple AI SaaS mesh gradients
- Dark neon “AI product” chrome
- Cream/terracotta calm-editorial autopilot
- Three equal feature cards and generic logo soup
- Cheerleader / pep-talk AI positioning

## Design Principles

1. Show the product (advisor challenge + documents), don’t decorate around it.
2. Challenge over cheer: copy and UI should feel like preparation for a hard room.
3. Clarity-first SaaS restraint (Linear / Mercury / Ramp class), Signal Teal as the brand accent.
4. One job per section; brand dominates the first viewport.
5. Motion only when it explains or confirms; never for noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Theme toggle supports light and dark; marketing defaults light-first.
